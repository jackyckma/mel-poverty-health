# Mandate

Topic-neutral slots filled for this instance. Items marked **(proposed default)** were not stated by the Owner; the Lead confirms them with the Owner in the first digests.

## Topic

Poverty and health

## Purpose

Monitor data and news at the junction of poverty and health. Produce charts, analysis and narratives on this topic to raise public awareness, using honest and proper methods.

## Audience

- Interested general public
- Academics working in this area

## Decisions informed

No direct decision-maker. Outputs are initial narratives and data views that journalists and interest groups may use in their own reporting and work. Because others may reuse them, every output must be traceable (source, date, method) and state its limits.

## Scope

- Geography: major cities in the US and Europe with population above 2 million.
- Theme: data and news linking poverty (income, deprivation, housing, inequality) with health (outcomes, access, determinants).
- City definition **(proposed default)**: use the definition each source uses (city proper or metro area), always label which one, and never mix definitions in one comparison without saying so. Curator maintains the city list in `data/` with the population source and year.

## Out of scope

**(proposed default)**

- Cities of 2 million or fewer, and regions outside the US and Europe, unless the Owner adds them.
- Individual-level or personally identifiable data.
- Medical advice, and causal claims that the data cannot support.
- Policy advocacy: the team informs, it does not campaign.

## Honest-method rules

- Every chart and claim cites source, date and geography.
- Show uncertainty and data gaps; do not hide missing cities or years.
- Correlation is not causation; say so where relevant.
- Do not compare figures with different definitions or years without flagging it.
- News items are signals, not evidence; prefer primary data.
- Corrections are logged openly.
