# Berlin: district health-and-social index, open CSV (C17); Madrid C16 year note

**C17 — Gesundheits- und Sozialstrukturatlas Berlin 2022** (Senate health department). Owner: SenWGPG. Format: CSV (1.6 KB for 12 districts; also 60 / 138 / 448 smaller areas). Update: periodic report (2013, 2022 editions; data years 2011, 2019). Licence: CC BY (datenregister.berlin.de metadata). No key.

Contains index z-scores only: overall GESIx plus sub-indices for labour market, social situation and health. First keyless Berlin source with a poverty side and a health side by district.

Caveat: these are composite scores from one method, so their correlation is partly built in. I suggest it as **context** for Berlin, not a headline poverty–health chart. Data year 2019, old area boundaries.

**C16 Madrid note:** the technical document does not state the life-expectancy year. Collector should check the Banco de datos de Madrid table before use.
