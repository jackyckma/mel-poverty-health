## 1. DWP Children in Low Income Families (CiLIF), local area statistics — London boroughs (child poverty)

- **Source:** Department for Work and Pensions, "Children in low income families: local area statistics, FYE 2022 to 2025". Published **2026-03-26**; next release **March 2027** (annual). Official Statistics. Page: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/children-in-low-income-families-local-area-statistics-2022-to-2025 (200). File: ODS on assets.publishing.service.gov.uk (3.2 MB, 200, no key). Fuller breakdowns on DWP Stat-Xplore (account/API key needed — not tried).
- **Geography:** UK region, local authority (London = 33 boroughs incl. City of London), parliamentary constituency, ward. London region and boroughs are in scope (city proper = Greater London).
- **Measure:** children aged 0–15 in families below 60% of median income; counts calibrated to the national HBAI survey total; rates use ONS mid-2024 population. Figures are **provisional** (DWP note 1). Two bases: **after housing costs (AHC)** and **before housing costs (BHC)**.
- **Values, FYE 2025 (FYE 2024 in brackets), % of children under 16:**

| Area | AHC relative | BHC relative |
|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 26.0 (26.3) | — |
| London region | 30.7 (31.7) | 15.6 (15.0) |
| Tower Hamlets (highest London borough) | 50.3 (52.4) | not extracted |
| Richmond upon Thames (lowest borough excl. City of London) | 10.6 (10.6) | not extracted |

- **Pattern worth a chart:** in London the AHC rate is about double the BHC rate (30.7% vs 15.6%) — housing costs are the main poverty driver for London children. Borough spread is about fivefold (10.6% to 50.3%).
- **Caveats:** the AHC local measure is not the same as the HBAI regional survey estimate — do not mix them. City of London is tiny (107 children); exclude from rankings. Provisional; may be revised in March 2027.
- **Licence:** GOV.UK statistics are normally under the Open Government Licence v3.0 — **not yet confirmed on this page**; Collector to confirm.
- **Why it matters:** first borough-level child-poverty series for London. It can sit beside borough health indicators we already have (Fingertips / C10 London Health Profile) for a poverty-and-health view at the same geography and year. Correlation only.
