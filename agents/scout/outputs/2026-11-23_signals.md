Short run. All checks were made directly from the Scout shell, about 07:00–07:40 UTC. These are signals, not evidence. Figures are as the publisher shows them, with source, date and geography.

## 1. Madrid life expectancy and income by district, one open file (new candidate C16)

- **Source:** Ayuntamiento de Madrid open data portal (CKAN), dataset `300087-0-indicadores-distritos`, "Panel de indicadores de distritos y barrios de Madrid". Licence **CC BY 4.0** (portal metadata). Updated 2026-10-20 (2026 panel edition). Annual.
- **Files:** cumulative CSV `.../resource/300087-0-indicadores-distritos-csv/download/acumulado.csv` (200, no key, **36 MB**, 134,657 rows, panels 2020–2026, `;`-separated, UTF-8). Single-edition XLSX `.../resource/300087-21-indicadores-distritos/download/panel2026.xlsx` (**2.5 MB**). Both are above the 2 MB fetch cap, so the Collector would need a raised cap for this source or a filtered copy.
- **Geography:** Madrid city proper (municipio, about 3.4 million), 21 districts and their barrios. Label as city proper.
- **Useful indicators in the same file:** "Renta media disponible por persona" (AEAT-based, latest year 2023); "Esperanza de vida al nacer" men and women (date field 01/01/2024; check whether this is 2023 mortality); "Tasa de riesgo de pobreza o exclusión social" (city level only, 7 rows); IGUALA vulnerability indices including a health sub-index; self-rated health from the Madrid Salud health survey; Renta mínima de inserción recipients.

| District (2026 panel) | Disposable income per person, 2023 (€) | Life expectancy at birth, men | Women |
|---|---|---|---|
| Puente de Vallecas (lowest income) | 12,056 | 81.2 (lowest) | 87.7 |
| Usera | 12,116 | 81.5 | 86.9 (lowest) |
| Madrid city | 19,632 | 83.5 | 88.3 |
| Salamanca | 29,687 | 85.0 (highest) | 89.4 |
| Chamartín (highest income) | 32,019 | 84.7 | 88.4 |

- Range across 21 districts: men 3.8 years, women 2.6 years. Spearman rank correlation of income with life expectancy across districts (our quick check, descriptive only): men 0.79, women 0.61.
- **Why it matters:** gives Madrid a poverty–health pairing like London (C11 + C15) and Paris (C12 + C13), from one publisher, one file and one district definition. It is also the first life-expectancy-by-district source we have for a European city.
- **Caveats:** 21 areas, ecological comparison only; correlation is not causation. The district life-expectancy figures have no confidence intervals in the file. Income and life expectancy refer to different years. The year label for life expectancy needs confirming from the technical document (`estructura_conjunto_datos_panel_indicadores_distritos_barrios-documento-tecnico.xlsx`). Some indicators come from different sources over time (e.g. the 2013–2016 Madrid Salud life-expectancy series is a separate indicator); do not mix them.
- **Related, same portal, CC BY 4.0:** deaths (`300610-0-defunciones-mapas`) and births (`300611-0-nacimientos-mapas`) by district and barrio, 2020–2025, as ZIP files. These could support a later infant-mortality check, but we would have to compute rates ourselves. Not proposed now.
- **Suggested owner:** Lead (proposal to the Owner); then Collector (licence check, size cap).
