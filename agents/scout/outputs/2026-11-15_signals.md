## C. Rome gap (partial)

- **Lazio DEP study** in *Epidemiologia & Prevenzione* (epiprev.it/5320): life expectancy at birth and at 65 by socioeconomic position across Rome's 15 health districts, 2006–2017. Reported gap: up to 3 years by neighbourhood, worst in the east (municipi IV, V, VI are also the lowest-income ones per the "Sviluppo Umano nei municipi di Roma" report).
- **Agenzia Roma** (agenzia.roma.it) yearly "Qualità della vita e dei servizi pubblici a Roma" surveys: perception data, not health outcomes.
- **Use:** narrative reference only. Data ends in 2017, the source is in Italian, and there are no district-level values in open form. Rome stays a **data** gap. Next: ISTAT city-level life expectancy (istat.it is reachable now).

## D. Definitions warning

NYC is city proper. LA is county. London is Greater London. Chicago food data may be Cook County. Do not put these side by side without labels. Homelessness counts follow different methods: NYC counts deaths among people known to services, LA uses coroner matching, and CHAIN counts people seen by outreach.
