## 1. INSEE Filosofi 2023 — Paris child poverty rate (new candidate C12)

- **Source:** INSEE, Fichier localisé social et fiscal (Filosofi) 2023, through the keyless **Melodi API** (`https://api.insee.fr/melodi/data/<dataset>?GEO=...`, 200, JSON, no key).
  - `DS_FILOSOFI_SAGE_LOG_TP_NIVVIE` gives the median standard of living and poverty rate by age group, sex and housing tenure. It covers departments and regions; the commune query returned 0 rows. Catalogue modified 2026-08-06.
  - `DS_FILOSOFI_CC` gives the standard of living and poverty rate with no age breakdown, by commune / department / region. Catalogue modified 2026-08-06.
- **Geography:** Paris is both a commune (75056) and a department (75), so the city proper is covered. Seine-Saint-Denis (93) and Île-de-France (REG-11) are available for context. Because Paris city and Paris department are the same area, the age breakdown is city-level.
- **Measure:** the poverty rate is the share of people below 60% of the median standard of living (`PR_MD60`). It covers tax households in ordinary dwellings. Year 2023.
- **Values, 2023, poverty rate at the 60% threshold (%):**

| Area | Under 18 | All ages |
|---|---|---|
| Paris (75) | 21.7 | 16.8 |
| Seine-Saint-Denis (93) | 38.7 | 29.5 |
| Île-de-France (region) | 23.9 | 17.3 |

- The Paris rate by tenure is 23.3% (TSH=2) and 6.1% (TSH=1). These codes probably mean tenants and owners; **the code list is not confirmed**.
- **Why it matters:** this is the Paris equivalent of C11 (DWP, London): a city-level child poverty rate from an official keyless API. It can sit beside health indicators for Paris. A later step could pair it with infant mortality by territory from the ORS report below. Correlation only.
- **Caveats:** a 60%-of-median threshold is used in both France and the UK, but income definitions differ. Filosofi uses no housing-cost adjustment, and DWP uses AHC/BHC modelled on a survey. **Do not rank London against Paris without a definitions note.** The 2020 figures in the ORS report (below) may not be strictly comparable with the 2023 figures here; INSEE methods can change between vintages.
- **Licence:** INSEE data are normally under the Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0. The data.gouv.fr Filosofi gridded datasets show `fr-lo` / `lov2`. This is **not confirmed for Melodi specifically**; the Collector should confirm it.
- **Suggested owner:** Lead, as a proposal to the Owner. If approved, the Collector confirms the licence and adds the source.

## 2. ORS Île-de-France, "La santé des enfants dans les territoires franciliens" (Sept 2024) — narrative / context

- **Source:** ORS Île-de-France, September 2024, PDF (hosted by ONPE, 200, 8.4 MB). It draws on SNDS, Insee Filosofi 2020 and Drees.
- **Content:**
  - Child poverty in 2020 by department: Paris 19.5% under 14, Seine-Saint-Denis 35.9%, Yvelines 14.2%, Île-de-France 21.5%.
  - Infant mortality in Île-de-France rose from 3.68‰ (2012) to 4.46‰ (2021).
  - Figure 5 plots infant mortality against the poverty rate across Île-de-France intercommunalities (2018 data).
  - The authors note that **child health data below department level are hard to get**.
- **Why it matters:** this is published, credible work linking child poverty and child health in the Paris region. It is useful as narrative context and as a methods reference. It is a report, not a dataset, so the primary data stays with INSEE / SNDS.
- **Suggested owner:** Lead / Narrative (cite as context only).
