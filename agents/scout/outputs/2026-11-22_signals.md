## 2. C13 follow-up — INSEE 3-year infant mortality window (likely centred)

- Melodi codelist (`/melodi/range/DS_DECES_MORTALITE_SERIES`) labels `MOR_INF_RT_3Y` "3-year smoothed infant mortality rate" and does **not** state the window. The dataset also has annual `MOR_INF_RT` (France only, not department).
- Compared the France 3-year series with the annual series, 2008–2023: a **centred** window (t−1 to t+1) matches better than trailing (mean absolute error 0.05 vs 0.08; e.g. "2023" = 4.0, centred 4.00, trailing 3.87). The 3-year series also stops at 2023 although annual 2024 is final and 2025 provisional, which fits a centred window.
- **So "2023" most likely means 2022–2024**, not 2021–2023 as guessed on 2026-11-21. Inferred, not confirmed by INSEE; label it as "3-year smoothed, centred on 2023 (inferred)" until confirmed.
