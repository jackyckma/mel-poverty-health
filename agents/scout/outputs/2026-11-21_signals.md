## 1. INSEE "Décès et mortalité – séries longues" — Paris life expectancy and infant mortality (new candidate C13)

- **Source:** INSEE, dataset `DS_DECES_MORTALITE_SERIES`, through the keyless **Melodi API** (`https://api.insee.fr/melodi/data/DS_DECES_MORTALITE_SERIES?GEO=DEP-75&EC_MEASURE=...`, 200, JSON, no key). Catalogue modified 2026-10-30. Catalogue `accessRights`: "Public / Free".
- **Geography:** department, region, France. Paris department (DEP-75) is the same area as Paris city proper, so the figures are city-level. Seine-Saint-Denis (DEP-93) and Île-de-France (REG-11) are available for context.
- **Measures found:** `LEXPEC` (life expectancy by age and sex; Paris from 1975), `MOR_INF_RT_3Y` (infant mortality rate, three-year rate, per 1,000 live births, 2008–2023), `MOR_RT`, `MOR_STANDARD_RT` (standardised mortality), `DTH_PLACE_RES` (deaths by place of residence).
- **Values:**

| Area | Life expectancy at birth 2025, F / M (years, provisional) | Infant mortality, 3-yr rate, "2023" (‰) |
|---|---|---|
| Paris (75) | 87.1 / 82.4 | 3.7 |
| Seine-Saint-Denis (93) | 85.7 / 81.0 | 5.7 |
| Île-de-France | 86.5 / 82.0 | 4.3 |
| Metropolitan France (`FRANCE-F`) | 85.9 / 80.3 | 4.0 |

- 2023–2025 life expectancy rows carry `OBS_STATUS = P` (provisional). The three-year window behind "2023" (probably 2021–2023) is **not confirmed**.
- **Why it matters:** with C12 (INSEE Filosofi child poverty, board 009) this gives Paris a matched pair from one publisher and one API: a poverty rate and two health outcomes for the same areas. Paris vs Seine-Saint-Denis is a clear poverty–health contrast (child poverty 21.7% vs 38.7%; infant mortality 3.7‰ vs 5.7‰). It also fills the "Paris infant mortality" gap from yesterday's next actions. Correlation only; two departments are not a sample.
- **Caveats:** small numbers make infant mortality noisy (hence the 3-yr rate). Do not compare with US infant mortality without a definitions note. Licence: Melodi says "Public / Free"; INSEE normally uses Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0; the Collector should confirm.
- **Suggested owner:** Lead (proposal to the Owner, together with C12); then Collector.
