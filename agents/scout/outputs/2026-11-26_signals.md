## Limits

- berlin.de returned **HTTP 429** after 3 PDF downloads (rate limit). The other 9 districts are not checked; values above come from Mitte, Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg and Spandau only. The Collector should fetch slowly (one file every few seconds).
- No licence or reuse statement found on the page or in the PDFs. The "Metadaten" and "Lesehilfe" PDFs could not be downloaded (429). Licence is **unknown** until the Collector checks.
- Reference years differ inside the profile (poverty 2019, life expectancy 2017/2019, mortality 2020). Any chart must label each year.
- The poverty rate is a regional (Berlin-relative or national-relative) threshold per the Mikrozensus; the Collector should confirm which, since it changes the meaning.
- District values are area averages: no individual-level inference, and correlation is not causation.
