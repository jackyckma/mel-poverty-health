## 1. Berlin district health-and-social index, open CSV (new candidate C17)

- **Source:** Senate health department (SenWGPG, health reporting unit), "Gesundheits- und Sozialstrukturatlas Berlin 2022" (GSSA 2022), via the Berlin open-data register (`datenregister.berlin.de`, CKAN API, no key). Licence **CC BY** (register metadata); the WFS/WMS map layers are dl-de-zero-2.0. Stand 03/2022; data year **2019** (2013 edition: data year 2011). Not annual — the atlas is a periodic report.
- **Files:** district CSV `https://www.berlin.de/sen/gesundheit/_assets/daten/gesundheits-und-sozialstrukturatlas/gssa_2022_bezirke.csv` (200, **1.6 KB**, UTF-8, 12 districts). Same columns also published for 60 Prognoseräume, 138 Bezirksregionen and 448 Planungsräume (old LOR areas, pre-2021). Metadata PDF `gssa_2022_metadaten.pdf` (42 KB).
- **Geography:** Berlin city proper (Land Berlin, about 3.7 million), 12 Bezirke. Label as city proper.
- **What is in it:** standardised index scores only (z-score style; higher = better situation), not raw indicators. Columns: GESIx (overall health-and-social index), ESIx, and three sub-indices — I Erwerbsleben (labour market), II Soziale Lage (social situation: Mikrozensus income, rents, school-entry exams, social assistance), III Gesundheit (health: hospital diagnoses, population statistics incl. mortality, registry). 20 indicators in all.
- **Quick look (2022 edition, data 2019):** lowest GESIx Neukölln −1.63, Spandau −1.17, Mitte −1.03; highest Steglitz-Zehlendorf 1.55, Pankow 1.19. Spearman across 12 districts, Soziale Lage vs Gesundheit sub-index 0.91; Erwerbsleben vs Gesundheit 0.94 (our quick check, descriptive only).
- **Why it matters:** first keyless, machine-readable Berlin source that puts a poverty dimension and a health dimension side by side by district. Fills the Berlin gap left open since 11-21 (London, Paris, Madrid pairs already proposed).
- **Caveats (important):** composite indices, not life expectancy or infant mortality; the sub-indices are built in one method, so their correlation is partly by construction and must not be presented as an independent poverty–health finding. Data 2019, pre-pandemic, and old LOR boundaries. 12 areas, ecological only; correlation is not causation. For a raw Berlin health indicator we still need GSI tables (not reachable from the shell) or Statistik Berlin-Brandenburg.
- **Suggested owner:** Lead (proposal; likely a context/secondary source rather than a headline chart); then Collector (licence check on the berlin.de asset).
